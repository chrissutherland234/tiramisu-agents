# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Assume good intent while discussing ideas, but evaluate technical and safety claims on evidence. Protect the privacy of clients, customers, contributors, and security reporters.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports should use the repository's private maintainer or security channels where confidentiality is appropriate.
